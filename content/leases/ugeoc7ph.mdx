## 1. Premises & Term

Tenant is renting the furnished two-bedroom, one-bath unit at 2351 Federal Blvd, Apt 402, Denver, CO 80211 (the "Premises") beginning September 9, 2026 through December 13, 2026 (the "Initial Term").

**Month-to-Month Conversion:** After December 13, 2026, this lease will automatically convert to a month-to-month tenancy under the same terms and conditions, except:

- Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice
- All other lease provisions remain in full effect during the month-to-month period

**Joint and Several Liability:** Kolby Perkins and Elizabeth White are each individually responsible for the full performance of this lease, including the full rent and all fees — not merely a pro-rata share — regardless of which of them made a given payment or caused a given charge.

## 2. Rent

**Amount:** $3,000 per month

**Due:** Paid monthly on the first of the month by 11:59 PM. Prorated for partial months.

**Payment Method:** Venmo (@lebraat), Zelle (dan.brunsdon@gmail.com), or another agreed app.

**Late Fee:** Rent is late if not received by the 8th of the month (a 7-day grace period from the due date, consistent with Colorado law). A one-time late fee of $150 applies to any month rent is received late — this is the statutory cap (the greater of $50 or 5% of monthly rent) and no additional daily or per-diem late fees will be charged on top of it.

**Returned Payment Fee:** $35 for any returned/failed payments.

**Cleaning Fee:** $200, deducted from the security deposit at move-out rather than charged separately — see Section 3.

## 3. Security Deposit

A refundable $1,000 security deposit is due upon signing. This will be returned within 30 days of move-out (as required by Colorado law), minus:

- The $200 cleaning fee (Section 2), deducted automatically
- Damage beyond normal wear and tear
- Unpaid rent or fees
- Cleaning costs beyond the flat fee above, if the unit requires more than normal cleaning
- Key replacement costs

Deductions will never include normal wear and tear, and any deduction will be documented with photos, receipts, or invoices, which Landlord will provide to Tenant within 14 days of a request. Tenant has the right to be present during the move-out inspection and may request a walk-through inspection in advance of move-out to identify any items that could otherwise be charged as damage.

## 4. Pets

Tenant may have up to 2 pets, provided they are house-trained and pre-approved by Landlord. A $150 non-refundable pet deposit per pet is required. Tenant is liable for all pet-related damage.

## 5. Utilities & Services
