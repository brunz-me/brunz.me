## Package Deliveries

Packages are delivered to either the mailroom or the Amazon Locker on the west side of the building, near the bike room entrance.

The community is safe and respectful, so you shouldn't have to worry about anyone going into the mailroom and taking a package. If something does go missing, let Daniel know and we can check the security camera.

## Bike Storage

You can store your bike in the bike room on the first floor. When I lived in the unit, I kept my bikes inside as an extra precaution since they're expensive — if you have any concerns about bikes getting stolen, it's better to play it safe and keep them in the unit.

## Trash & Recycling

The building has both trash and recycling, but no compost yet.

The trash (green) and recycling (tan) dumpsters are by the front gate entrance, and are picked up Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

## Heating & Cooling

Heating and air conditioning are controlled separately.

- Heat: Nest thermostat in the living room, by the patio door. It typically doesn't get switched on for the season until early October.
- Cooling: wall unit near the bathroom.

## Parking

This unit comes with parking spot #20. A second car will either need to park on the street or rent an additional spot for $150/month, if one's available.

Please don't park in the guest spots yourselves — but guests visiting you are welcome to.

## Streaming & Speakers

If you connect a streaming service during your stay, please disconnect it before you move out — otherwise a future resident might end up with access to your account (and a slightly stranger recommendation algorithm).

You can connect to the custom-built speakers by the TV: make sure the device in the TV console is powered on, then pair over Bluetooth.

Noise doesn't travel into the unit much, but it does travel between floors — please be mindful of your neighbors below.

## Points of Interest

Meow Wolf, Mile High Stadium, Elitch Gardens, the Denver Aquarium, and Ball Arena are all close by. Also worth a walk: Sloan's Lake, or down to the South Platte River and riverfront area near REI.

The MCA is very close, and the Denver Art Museum is a great visit too. Westword magazine is a solid source for more recommendations.

Favorites nearby: Briar Common, 2914 Coffee, Santiago's, and Snarf's Sandwiches. Beyond that, anything in the Highlands, LoHi, or RiNo is a good bet.
